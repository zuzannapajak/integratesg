## Company overview

**Name:** Polski Koncern Naftowy Orlen S.A. (PKN Orlen)

**Sector:** Energy, Refining, and Petrochemicals

**Location:** Płock, Poland

**Size:** Central Europe's largest refining and petrochemical company, integrated energy group

**Reporting Year:** 2023

Polski Koncern Naftowy Orlen S.A. (PKN Orlen) is the largest integrated energy and petrochemical company in Central Europe, headquartered in Płock, Poland. The company operates across the oil, gas, refining, petrochemical and retail fuel sectors, with a presence in over 100 countries. Orlen manages refineries, a vast network of fuel stations and is expanding into renewable energy and hydrogen production as part of its energy transition strategy. Listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, PKN Orlen is a key player in Poland’s economy and a leader in the region’s move toward sustainable energy solutions.

PKN Orlen demonstrates a "triple integration" model: (1) ESG integrated into business strategy, (2) business strategy integrated with decarbonisation roadmap and (3) all three aligned with international frameworks (Paris Agreement, UN SDGs, European Green Deal).

## ESG integration

PKN Orlen's ESG integration is comprehensive and strategically embedded, with sustainability as the driving force behind the ORLEN2030 strategy. The integrated approach enables the company to capture opportunities from environmental changes while executing a socially responsible energy transition, with clear targets, substantial financial commitments and alignment with international frameworks.

## Recommendations

Accelerated renewable energy portfolio diversification: while PKN Orlen has committed PLN 30 billion to sustainability projects and set a 2050 net-zero target, the company should accelerate its renewable energy capacity buildout with specific interim targets for 2030 (e.g., 5 GW renewable capacity) and 2040, diversifying beyond traditional refining into offshore wind, green hydrogen production at scale, and battery storage infrastructure. This would reduce transition risk exposure and position Orlen as a true multi-energy leader rather than a transitioning fossil fuel company.

Science-based scope 3 reduction targets: although PKN Orlen tracks Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions comprehensively, the company should establish SBTi-validated targets specifically for Scope 3 emissions (which represent the majority of its carbon footprint from end-use of petroleum products), with concrete strategies to support customer decarbonisation through low-carbon fuel alternatives, carbon capture partnerships, and circular economy initiatives. This would address the most material climate impact and enhance credibility with ESG investors.

Enhanced biodiversity and circular economy integration: beyond climate focus, PKN Orlen should develop explicit biodiversity protection targets aligned with the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework and implement circular economy principles across petrochemical operations, including chemical recycling capacity for plastics, closed-loop water systems and zero-waste-to-landfill commitments by 2030. This would address emerging regulatory requirements (EU Nature Restoration Law) and differentiate Orlen in environmental stewardship beyond carbon.

Stakeholder-inclusive just transition framework: as Orlen transforms its business model, the company should establish a formal Just Transition framework with dedicated funding, reskilling programs for workers in declining fossil fuel operations, community economic diversification support in refinery-dependent regions, and transparent social impact assessments for all major strategic shifts. This would mitigate social risks, strengthen community relations and align with EU Just Transition Mechanism principles, ensuring no stakeholder is left behind in the energy transition.
