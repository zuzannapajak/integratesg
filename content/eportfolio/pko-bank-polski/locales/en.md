## Company Overview

**Name:** PKO Bank Polski S.A. (Powszechna Kasa Oszczędności Bank Polski Spółka Akcyjna)

**Sector:** Banking and Financial Services

**Location:** Warsaw, Poland

**Size:** Poland's largest bank with 1,145+ branches, assets of 501.5 billion PLN (2023)

**Reporting Year:** 2023-2024

PKO Bank Polski S.A. is Poland's largest bank and a leading financial institution in Central and Eastern Europe, headquartered in Warsaw. With over 100 years of history, the bank serves millions of retail and institutional customers through an extensive distribution network of over 1,100 branches. As of 2023, PKO BP holds the largest market share in the Polish banking sector (20%), with leading positions in loans (17%), mortgage lending (20.4%) and investment funds for individuals (20.6%). The bank employs approximately 29,000 people and operates through a diversified Capital Group offering comprehensive banking, insurance, leasing, factoring and investment services. PKO Bank Polski is listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange and reported a net profit exceeding 5.5 billion PLN in 2023.

## ESG practices integrated and implementation methods

The integration approach is systematic and embedded across strategy, operations, risk management, product development and reporting – positioning ESG not as a separate initiative but as a core business driver aligned with long-term value creation.

### Environmental Practices

Green finance and product development:

- Expanded product offerings supporting sustainable development and transformation projects

- Green bonds and covered bonds for financing environmental projects

- Green structured deposits and sustainability-linked loans

- Transactions on CO2 emission rights market

- Gradual increase in green products for retail, corporate, leasing and enterprise clients

Operational environmental management:

- Energy efficiency improvements across branch network

- Transition to renewable energy sources for operations

- Digital transformation reducing paper consumption and physical infrastructure needs

- Reduced CO2 emissions by over 60% (2019-2021)

- Reduced A4 paper consumption by 65% over five years

- Target to achieve climate neutrality in Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2025

- Reduced involvement in high-emission industries to 0.5%

### Green finance portfolio

- Development of green loan products for retail and corporate clients

- Financing renewable energy projects and energy-efficient building renovations

- Integration of environmental risk assessment in credit decision processes

ESG risk integration:

- ESG assessment for all lending activities evaluating both loan impact on ESG issues and ESG factors' impact on loans

- Credit assessment incorporates environmental impact of business activities and compliance with environmental permits

### Social Practices

- Support for diversity and equal treatment of employees regardless of age, gender and origin

- Respect for all stakeholders as core of operational model

### Employee development (#ESGo Program)

Customer support:

## Recommendations for improvement

**Enhanced climate target ambition:** While PKO Bank Polski has achieved significant emissions reductions and targets climate neutrality by 2025 for Scope 1 and 2, the bank should establish science-based targets validated by the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) that include comprehensive Scope 3 financed emissions with clear interim milestones for 2030 and 2040. This would strengthen credibility with ESG-focused investors and align with Paris Agreement goals.

**Expanded ESG product portfolio:** The bank should accelerate development of innovative green finance products beyond traditional green bonds, including sustainability-linked loans with pricing tied to borrower ESG performance, green mortgages with preferential rates for energy-efficient homes and ESG-themed investment funds accessible to retail customers. Additionally, developing advisory services to help SME clients navigate their own ESG transitions would create competitive differentiation and deepen client relationships.

**Supplier ESG due diligence:** Although ESG criteria are integrated into procurement, PKO should implement systematic ESG audits of its top 100 suppliers with transparent scorecards and improvement programs, ensuring the entire value chain aligns with the bank's sustainability commitments. This would demonstrate leadership in responsible supply chain management.

**Stakeholder engagement and transparency:** The bank should establish an external ESG advisory council comprising independent experts, NGO representatives and academics to provide critical oversight and guidance on sustainability strategy, while also publishing quarterly ESG performance updates and standalone sustainability reports with third-party assurance.
