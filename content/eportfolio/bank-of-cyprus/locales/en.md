## Company Overview

**Name:** Bank of Cyprus Holdings Public Limited Company

**Sector:** Financials (Financial Services)

**Location:** Strovolos, Nicosia, Cyprus (Headquarters: 51 Stassinos Street, Ayia Paraskevi, 2002 Nicosia)

**Size:** Approx. 2,850 employees, 56 branches

**Reporting Year:** 2025 (Annual Financial Report for the year ended 31 December 2025)

Founded in 1899, Bank of Cyprus Public Company Limited is the leading financial services group in Cyprus, offering a comprehensive suite of products across retail and commercial banking, wealth management, insurance, and corporate finance. It operates as the country's primary financial institution, holding a dominant market share and supporting local consumer and business economic activity. The bank is actively driving digital banking innovation and leading Cyprus's transition toward a sustainable, net-zero economy.

## ESG practices integrated and implementation methods

### Environmental Practices

The "Our Environment" strategy of Bank of Cyprus centers on leading the green transition in Cyprus by serving as a role model through its own operations while actively assisting its customers in achieving their net-zero ambitions. To support the broader shift toward a Net Zero Economy, the bank has committed to aligning its portfolio's financed emissions with net-zero goals by 2050. As part of this effort, it joined the Partnership for Carbon Accounting Financials (PCAF) to measure Scope 3 greenhouse gas emissions linked to its loans and investments, while setting sector-specific decarbonization targets aligned with global climate scenarios.

In its internal management, Bank of Cyprus focuses on achieving carbon neutrality across its own operations. The institution has evaluated its direct greenhouse gas emissions and implemented an energy-efficiency strategy targeting full operational carbon neutrality by 2050. As an interim milestone toward this goal, the bank has committed to reducing its absolute Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions by 42% by 2030, using 2021 as its baseline year.

Beyond corporate and financial operations, environmental stewardship is integrated into the bank's broader Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) framework as one of its five core pillars. Bank of Cyprus actively establishes strategic partnerships to address climate change by prioritizing four main action areas: raising public awareness and environmental education, promoting disaster prevention and response, mitigating climate change consequences, and actively supporting the protection of local biodiversity.

### Social Practices

The Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) strategy at Bank of Cyprus is grounded in the overarching philosophy of the "Common of Cypriots," positioning the institution as an active, long-term partner dedicated to strengthening public welfare and empowering society. The bank structures its social initiatives across core strategic focus areas: Health, Education, Environment, Culture, Entrepreneurship, and community-wide emergency response. Through targeted funding, strategic alliances, and volunteer engagements, the framework aims to address fundamental social challenges, promote sustainable development, and reduce inequalities across Cyprus.

Key operational commitments within these pillars include sustained support for health and welfare initiatives, most notably through its long-standing partnership with the Bank of Cyprus Oncology Centre to advance cancer care, support patients and their families, and run public health campaigns. In education and entrepreneurship, the bank allocates financial and material resources toward financial literacy programs, STEAM talent awards, and youth sports sponsorships, while driving economic innovation through its IDEA Innovation Center, which provides start-ups with business creation training, professional mentoring, and seed funding. Additionally, the Cultural Foundation preserves Cyprological heritage by managing museums, curated collections, and public educational programs.

To address urgent crisis situations and reinforce its strategic CSR pillars, Bank of Cyprus established SupportCY, a collaborative network linking corporate partners, a dedicated Volunteer Corps, and a Crises and Disasters Centre. This initiative mobilizes rapid-response humanitarian aid, fire and flood prevention programs, and community relief projects during emergencies. Through these combined efforts across health, education, cultural preservation, and crisis management, the bank maintains an integrated framework for delivering measurable, positive social impact throughout the island.

### Governance Practices

The Sustainability Governance framework at Bank of Cyprus is structured to align organizational practices with regulatory standards, global best practices, and evolving Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) expectations. Ultimate accountability for identifying, assessing, and integrating ESG impacts, climate-related risks, and sustainability opportunities across the group rests with the Board of Directors. To ensure continuous and specialized oversight, the Board relies on both internal operational capabilities and external expertise to assess climate stability and long-term socio-economic value creation.

Board oversight is primarily executed through delegated authority to specialized committees, most notably the Nominations and Corporate Governance Committee (NCGC) and the Risk Committee. The NCGC monitors the group's general ESG and climate strategy to promote economic prosperity, environmental integrity, and social equity. Simultaneously, the Risk Committee oversees the identification, assessment, and control of financial and non-financial risks, explicitly including physical and transition climate-related and environmental (C&E) risks. Additionally, the Audit Committee assesses the soundness, methodologies, and internal control systems governing the bank's overall ESG framework, including oversight of the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) reporting processes.

At the executive level, the Executive Committee (EXCO) recommends non-risk ESG and climate policies directly to the Board via the NCGC. Operationally, a dedicated management-level Sustainability Committee reports directly to the EXCO, while the Risk Management Division evaluates ESG risk policies and submits them straight to the Board’s Risk Committee. This integrated dual-track governance structure ensures that operational ESG initiatives and specialized risk management protocols are reviewed and embedded into the group's broader corporate strategy.

## Information on indices scoring

**MSCI ESG Ratings:** Maintains an 'A' rating. As specified in your guidelines, this letter rating indicates how the bank manages ESG risks relative to its global banking peers.

## Success in regulatory compliance

Fully compliant with the EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) and the EU Taxonomy, ensuring that its "Green" claims meet strict European technical screening criteria.

## Recommendations for improvement

Enhance disclosure regarding Scope 3 financed emissions to provide investors with more granular data on the environmental impact of its entire lending portfolio.
