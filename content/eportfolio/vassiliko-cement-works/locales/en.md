## Company Overview

**Name:** Vassiliko Cement Works Public Company Ltd.

**Sector:** Materials / Heavy Construction Materials (Cement & Building Materials Production)

**Location:** 1A Kyriakos Matsis Avenue, 1082 Nicosia, Cyprus (Registered Headquarters), Main Production Plant & Industrial Port: Vassiliko, Mari, Larnaca, Cyprus

**Size:** ~330+ employees

**Reporting Year:** 2025 (Annual Report and Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025)

Established in 1963, Vassiliko Cement Works Public Company Ltd is one of the largest heavy industrial and manufacturing enterprises in Cyprus, specializing in the large-scale production and distribution of high-grade clinker and cement. Listed on the Cyprus Stock Exchange, the company supplies both the domestic construction sector and international markets through its fully integrated, state-of-the-art production plant and dedicated port facilities at Vassiliko. The organization is actively advancing industrial decarbonization, focusing on alternative fuels, waste heat recovery, and photovoltaic energy production to reduce its operational carbon footprint.

## ESG practices integrated and implementation methods

### Environmental Practices

Environment Oriented Development strategy on balancing industrial operations with environmental stewardship, embedding the creation of a clean, sustainable environment into its core mission and corporate culture. Over the past six years, the company has made significant capital investments in major infrastructure projects that adhere to European standards to improve overall environmental performance, boost operational capability, and lower production costs. These initiatives have delivered substantial environmental outcomes, including a 15-20% drop in carbon dioxide emissions, a 30% reduction in thermal power consumption, a 30% cut in electricity used for cement production, and a 70% decrease in overall water consumption, alongside the installation of cutting-edge filter technologies that drastically minimize kiln dust emissions.

Operates under a strict Health & Safety Policy that treats worker safety with the same operational weight as productivity, a critical factor for the high-risk industrial sector. It demonstrates its social commitment by actively supporting local communities, vulnerable social groups, and educational initiatives through targeted philanthropic and civic engagements. The company collaborates with organizations such as the Pancyprian Volunteerism Coordinative Council, the Sophia Foundation for Children, PASYKAF, and the Karaiskakio Foundation to sponsor programs including "Adopt a Family," "I Adopt a Child," and back-to-school drives. Beyond direct welfare support, Vassiliko invests in community development by funding local cultural events, youth chess tournaments, and educational workshops—such as its established "First Aid to Children" program and joint environmental education initiatives with CY.C.E.R.E.. Furthermore, the company prioritizes health, safety, and well-being by hosting awareness campaigns on diabetes, drug abuse prevention, and workplace safety, reinforcing a human-centered approach across both its workforce and neighboring local communities.

Implemented a dedicated Supply Chain Policy that requires vendors to adhere to sustainable development and corporate responsibility standards, effectively extending the company's ESG principles to its partners. It structures its Corporate Governance framework around a comprehensive set of rules and practices enforced by its Board of Directors to ensure accountability, equal treatment, and transparency across all business stakeholders—including shareholders, financial associates, customers, management, regulatory bodies, and the broader community. Under this framework, the Board is responsible for defining the company's long-term strategic objectives, providing ongoing oversight for their implementation, and regularly publishing detailed annual Corporate Governance and Remuneration Reports to maintain disclosure standards. To enforce strict oversight and risk management, the governance structure operates with specialized Board Committees, including an Audit Committee, a Nominations Committee, and a Remuneration Committee, alongside a designated Senior Independent Director to safeguard balanced decision-making and ethical compliance.

## Social Practices

Occupational Health & Safety (OH&S): Operates under ISO 45001 certification, maintaining strict safety protocols across industrial plant facilities and heavy port operations, with continuous hazard assessments and employee safety training.

Employee Education & Skill Development: Conducts regular upskilling programs and technical training for technical and administrative staff, supporting career progression and lifelong learning.

Community Engagement & "Summer School": Organizes educational programs such as annual Summer Schools and local youth initiatives aimed at students in neighboring communities (e.g., Mari, Kalavasos, Zygi) to foster learning, sports, and skill development.

Local Community Support & Welfare: Partners with local municipalities in the Vassiliko industrial region to fund local infrastructure, environmental enhancement projects, and community welfare funds.

### Governance Practices

Board Committees & Oversight: Maintains specialized Board Committees—such as Audit, Risk, and Remuneration—to monitor corporate decision-making, financial reporting, and compliance.

Senior Independent Director: Appoints a Senior Independent Director to safeguard shareholder rights, ensure objective Board governance, and facilitate independent communication between board members and investors.

ISO-Certified Integrated Management Systems: Adheres to international standards across operations, including ISO 9001 (Quality Management), ISO 14001 (Environmental Management), ISO 50001 (Energy Management), and ISO 45001 (Health & Safety).

Transparent Stakeholder Disclosures: Publishes annual standalone Corporate Governance and Remuneration Reports detailing executive compensation, internal risk controls, shareholder rights, and regulatory compliance status.

Policy Enforcement: Enforces formal corporate governance policies across its value chain, including a dedicated Supply Chain Policy, Anti-Bribery & Whistleblowing guidelines, and strict Code of Conduct regulations.

## Information on indices scoring

Included in the Cyprus Stock Exchange (CSE) ESG Index, which monitors the performance of listed companies based on their ESG disclosure quality.

## Success in regulatory compliance

Complies with heavy-industry standards under the [EU Emissions Trading System (EU ETS)](https://climate.ec.europa.eu/areas-action/carbon-markets/eu-emissions-trading-system-eu-ets_en) and implements environmental management systems aligned with ISO 14001.

## Recommendations for improvement

Increase the replacement rate of traditional clinker with low-carbon alternatives to meet the tightening "Fit for 55" EU regulatory targets.
