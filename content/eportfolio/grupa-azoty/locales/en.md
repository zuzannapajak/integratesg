## Company Overview

**Name:** Grupa Azoty S.A.

**Sector:** Chemicals and Fertilizers

**Location:** Tarnów, Poland

**Size:** Poland's largest chemical company, leading European fertilizer producer

**Reporting Year:** 2023

Grupa Azoty S.A. is the leader of the Polish fertilizer market and one of the largest fertilizer-chemical capital groups in Europe. Headquartered in Poland and listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange since 2008, the company operates a conglomerate of over 50 companies both domestically and internationally. Its core business focuses on fertilizers, but it also has significant operations in chemical logistics, defence-related chemistry and advanced chemicals. Grupa Azoty produces around 5 million tonnes of fertilizers annually, manages a logistics capacity of 8.2 million tonnes and operates its own extensive railway infrastructure, solidifying its position as the second-largest fertilizer producer in the EU and a key player in the European chemical industry.

## ESG practices integrated and implementation methods

Grupa Azoty demonstrates comprehensive ESG integration through its "Zielone Azoty" strategic framework, combining environmental innovation (ISCC+ certified bio-based products, ISO 14001 systems), social responsibility (15,000+ employees, farmer education, scientific partnerships) and strong governance (WIG ESG index membership, UN SDG partnership, NFRD compliance, Good Practices adherence), positioning the company as a sustainability leader in the European chemical and fertilizer industry.

## Success in regulatory compliance

Grupa Azoty demonstrates comprehensive regulatory compliance across the complex EU chemical industry framework, with particular strength in REACH and CLP compliance, IED and ETS environmental requirements, ISCC+ certification for sustainable products (2022), ISO 14001:2015 environmental management systems, full NFRD reporting, UN SDG partnership since 2020 and adherence to Warsaw Stock Exchange "Good Practices 2021," positioning the company as a compliance leader in the European fertilizer and chemical sector despite operating in a highly regulated, carbon-intensive industry.

## Recommendations for improvement

Grupa Azoty S.A. should set more ambitious, science-based targets for reducing Scope 1, 2 and especially Scope 3 greenhouse gas emissions, with clear interim milestones and transparent progress reporting. The company can further enhance its ESG profile by expanding supplier sustainability audits and integrating stricter environmental and social criteria into procurement processes. Increasing investment in circular economy solutions – such as chemical recycling and closed-loop water systems – will help reduce waste and resource consumption. Finally, Grupa Azoty should strengthen stakeholder engagement by publishing more frequent, detailed ESG updates and fostering open dialogue with local communities, employees and investors.
