## Company Overview

**Name:** Allegro S.A.

**Sector:** E-commerce and Technology

**Location:** Poznań, Poland

**Size:** Poland's largest e-commerce platform, serving millions of customers

**Reporting Year:** 2023

Allegro S.A. is the undisputed leader of the Polish e-commerce market and one of the largest online marketplaces in Europe. Operating primarily as a third-party platform, it connects millions of active buyers with over a hundred thousand merchants offering a vast array of products across multiple retail categories. Beyond its core marketplace, Allegro has built a comprehensive digital ecosystem featuring the highly successful "Allegro Smart!" subscription model, proprietary logistics networks including parcel lockers and integrated fintech solutions like Allegro Pay. Headquartered in Poznań and listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, the company is actively expanding its footprint across Central and Eastern Europe to solidify its regional dominance.

## ESG practices integrated and implementation methods

Allegro S.A. demonstrates comprehensive ESG integration with a sophisticated three-tier governance structure, board-level oversight, ambitious renewable energy targets (50% by 2030, 100% as infrastructure allows), transparent CDP reporting since FY18 and strategic embedding of sustainability into product development, operations and supply chain management, positioning the company as a leader in semiconductor industry sustainability.

## Information on indices scoring

The company’s inclusion in multiple prestigious indices, particularly WIG-ESG, reflects strong performance across commercial, operational and sustainability dimensions. Allegro is actively monitored by major global ESG rating agencies and consistently ranks highly within the e-commerce and retail sectors.

- Member of WIG-ESG index, Warsaw Stock Exchange's ESG index, which highlights companies with the highest environmental, social and governance standards based on Sustainalytics scoring

- MSCI ESG Rating: AA (Leader) – Allegro was upgraded to an 'AA' rating (on a scale of AAA to CCC), placing it in the "Leader" category for the retail and e-commerce sector. This reflects strong data privacy, cybersecurity measures and carbon mitigation strategies.

- Sustainalytics ESG Risk Rating: Low Risk – Allegro consistently achieves a "Low Risk" score (typically around 11.0 - 13.0), indicating a low probability of experiencing material financial impacts from ESG factors. It ranks in the top percentiles globally for the retail/e-commerce subindustry.

- CDP (Carbon Disclosure Project): B Score (Management Level) – Allegro actively participates in the CDP Climate Change questionnaire, achieving a 'B' score, which indicates the company is taking coordinated action on climate issues and effectively managing its environmental impact.

- S&P Global Corporate Sustainability Assessment (CSA) – Allegro actively participates in the S&P Global CSA, scoring well above the industry average for the Retailing sector, particularly excelling in governance, customer relationship management and privacy protection.

## Recommendations for improvement

Allegro S.A. should accelerate its transition to renewable energy by setting clear, time-bound targets for 100% green electricity across all operations and logistics. The company can further strengthen its ESG profile by expanding supplier audits and integrating stricter environmental and social criteria into procurement processes. Enhancing transparency through more frequent, detailed ESG reporting – especially on Scope 3 emissions and supply chain impacts – will build greater stakeholder trust. Finally, Allegro should invest in digital accessibility and inclusive design to ensure its platform is fully usable by people with disabilities, anticipating upcoming EU accessibility regulations.
